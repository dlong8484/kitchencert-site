Restore a purchase

Open KitchenCert, tap the settings button on the Home tab, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, choose KitchenCert, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. The Pass Pack is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The AI tutor isn’t available

The tutor uses Apple Intelligence, which requires a supported iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro or later) with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Every question in KitchenCert still includes a verified explanation without the tutor.

Your data

KitchenCert keeps your progress on your device and doesn’t collect personal information. Read the Privacy Policy.

Which exams does KitchenCert prepare for?

KitchenCert’s original questions are written from the FDA Food Code and cover the 7 content areas of ANAB-accredited Certified Food Protection Manager exams. It is an independent study tool and isn’t affiliated with any exam provider.
